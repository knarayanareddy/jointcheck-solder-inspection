# B-T24-T21 Blocker — threshold evidence and rule-contract mismatch

## Precise question

May the requested values be marked `human_reviewed: true` and applied to production despite the absence of source citations/calibration data and despite the requested rule IDs and metrics not matching the frozen gate contracts?

**Decision made for this implementation:** no. Keep the production rules unchanged and fail closed until a human supplies reviewable evidence and the rule/schema mapping is resolved.

## What was tried and observed

- Inspected `data/workmanship-rules.v0.json` and `backend/app/gate/workmanship-rules.v0.json`; they are identical. Metadata identifies the thresholds as unsourced provisional placeholders, and the rules have `human_reviewed: false`.
- Found actual controlled rules `JC-WET-001`, `JC-EXC-001`, and `JC-BRD-001`; the requested `RULE-WETTING-01`, `RULE-SOLDER-01`, and `RULE-BRIDGE-01` are absent. The checked-in rules do not contain a per-rule `provisional` field.
- Compared metrics with `schemas/core-schemas.json`: wetting currently uses coverage, excess uses solder-volume ratio, bridge uses a numeric gap; `fillet_concavity` and boolean `bridge_detected` are not Measurement quantities. The current gate accepts one numeric threshold metric, not the requested mixed/two-metric or boolean criteria.
- Checked `data/testset-protocol.md` and the workspace: there is no `data/testset/` corpus or `eval/` calibration implementation. The protocol requires labelled/calibrated evidence; synthetic tests cannot replace it.
- Implemented and verified the safe portion of B-T21: a self-contained printable HTML renderer that embeds thumbnails and hashes the generated bytes. No Sandbox SDK or guest-network-disabled operation was available or invoked.
- Full backend verification passed: 42 tests. The new calibration tests verify the unsupported production rule IDs abstain rather than fabricate a verdict.

## Two options

1. **Recommended:** provide/record authoritative references and a human review decision; reconcile the IDs/metrics in a separately authorized schema/rule change; acquire the double-labelled data and perform B-T24 calibration. Then add production verdict tests and wire only reviewed rules to trusted measurements.
2. Keep the current rule table unreviewed and the inspection endpoint `UNDETERMINED`; retain the standalone HTML report generator as a prototype-only artifact and defer threshold-based production grading.

This is a missing-evidence and frozen-contract blocker, not a claim that three agent runs were exhausted. Further coding-only iterations cannot establish the requested provenance or calibration results.
