# Devpost demo feedback — collection plan and honest status

## Status

**No participant or field feedback has been collected for this demo in this repository.** There are no interview quotes, survey results, endorsements, usability metrics, customer deployments, or production evaluations to report. Do not present this plan as completed research.

## Feedback goals

If feedback is collected later, separate usability observations about the interface from technical validation of inspection performance. A participant's positive reaction is not evidence that the measurements, thresholds, or production decisions are correct.

## Suggested protocol

1. Obtain informed consent and follow the applicable privacy, company, and event rules before showing or collecting any data.
2. Use only synthetic or explicitly authorized images in a usability session. Do not upload confidential board imagery to an external service.
3. Explain before the task that diagrams are synthetic, scenario annotations are not measurements, and the prototype has no validated production grade.
4. Ask participants to complete neutral tasks (find the offline scenes, change the example class, generate and open the HTML report, and explain what the checksum does).
5. Ask open questions about clarity, accessibility, uncertainty, and potentially misleading visual cues; do not solicit a desired endorsement.
6. Record date, context, consent status, task outcomes, and limitations. De-identify notes and quote only with explicit permission.
7. Keep expert review of measurement rules, calibration, and labelled validation data as a separate workstream with documented methods.

## Reporting rule

Never turn synthetic examples, repository tests, a successful static build, or a demo recording into claims about user trust, inspection accuracy, IPC-A-610 compliance, production readiness, edge latency, or field performance. Attribute any future comments, distinguish opinion from measured outcomes, and disclose sample size and method.
